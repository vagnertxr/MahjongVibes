# Sound effects

These sound effects are from Kenney's [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)
and [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) packs, dedicated to the
public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No
attribution is required, but credit is given here for reference.

- `discard.ogg` — Casino Audio, `chip-lay-2.ogg`
- `call.ogg` — Casino Audio, `chips-collide-2.ogg`
- `riichi.ogg` — Interface Sounds, `confirmation_002.ogg`
- `win.ogg` — Interface Sounds, `confirmation_004.ogg`
- `shuffle.ogg` — Casino Audio, `card-shuffle.ogg`
